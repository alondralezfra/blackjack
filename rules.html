The Rules of Blackjack

Blackjack is a game where the goal is to get as close to 21 as possible without going over. If you go over 21, you "bust" and lose the game.

Basic Rules

  • The game is played with one or more decks of standard playing cards.
  • Each player is dealt two cards, and the dealer also receives two cards (one face up and one face down).
  • Players can choose to "hit" (take another card) or "stand" (keep their current hand).
  • If a player's hand exceeds 21, they bust and lose the game.
